Report a quest

Met quests Lorebound doesn't know, or read quests whose Forever text or rewards it hasn't got? The journal notes them as you play. Send them here and they join the quest list for everyone.

  1. In game, open the journal (/lb) and press Share quests on the overview.
  2. Copy the code with Ctrl+C and paste it below.
  3. Press Send, then Mark as shared in game.

Only the quest lines are sent: the quest, its zone and level, who gives it and takes it in, and the quest text and rewards you read (your character's name is left out). Each report becomes a public issue on GitHub, read before anything from it is added; every quest is also checked against the game's own quest list and Wowhead.
