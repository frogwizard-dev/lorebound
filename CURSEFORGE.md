# Lorebound

Lorebound tracks every quest in WoW Forever, zone by zone, and shows what's left for your character: what you can pick up now, what comes next and what it's waiting for, and what you're locked out of.

## The journal

- Open it with `/lb`, a key of your choice in Key Bindings, or LT + L3 on a controller.
- Zones run down the left, each with its own ring and its map behind it. Pin the ones you're working on.
- A zone's page has three rings over its fully revealed map: the zone, its continent and your whole Lorebound count.
- Its quests are sorted the way you'd use them: in your quest log, available now, coming up, done, and locked out.
- Click a quest for its page: objectives, story, its storyline and Forever's own rewards (the choices, items, money and experience, as Forever's servers give them).
- Search every quest by name, and resize or scale the window from its corner.

## Knows when you're locked out

Breadcrumbs you skipped past, pairs where you could only do one, chains you joined partway: Lorebound works out which quests you can no longer get and tells you why. Quests you moved past count as done, so a zone can still be finished.

## Quest details

Open a quest for its objective, its text from when you last read it, and who gives it. One click shows the quest giver on a map of the zone and puts a map pin link in chat.

## Rings, badges and toasts

- Each quest you hand in shows a toast with the zone's ring ticking up.
- Close a ring and you earn its badge: the zone's own map in a gold frame. Dungeons and raids show their painting.
- Each continent has a crest, and finishing them all earns the Lorebound Seal.
- Click a badge to look at it up close. Drag it to spin it.

## Learns as you play

When you meet a quest that isn't in its list, Lorebound adds it as you go. Many quests changed in Forever, and Lorebound keeps their text as you read it too. Share both from the journal at lb.frogwizard.net/report and they join the list for everyone (your character's name is left out of the text).

## On your phone

Type `/lb export` and paste the code at lb.frogwizard.net to see your rings, badges and every quest's status in a browser. Save it to a private link with a QR code to keep it on your phone while you play. The site also lists every quest with its text, rewards and map.

## Controller

Works with the gamepad UI: the D-pad moves, A opens, B goes back, X shows the quest giver, LB and RB change the filter.

## Commands

- `/lb` or `/lorebound`: open or close the journal
- `/lb <zone>`: open a zone (the start of its name is enough)
- `/lb export`: your progress code for the website
- `/lb reset`: the window back to its first size and place
